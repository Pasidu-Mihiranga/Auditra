# Auditra Design System: Color Palette & Harmony

The Auditra project uses a **Professional Slate & Blue** color palette designed to evoke trust, precision, and modern efficiency. This design system is shared across both the Web Dashboard and the Mobile Application.

## 🎨 The Color Palette

| Role | Hex Code | Visual Name | Purpose |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Primary** | `#1565C0` | **Denim Blue** | Brand identity, main buttons, and active states. |
| **Secondary** | `#60A5FA` | **Sky Blue** | Accents, toggles, and supporting interactive elements. |
| **Background** | `#F1F5F9` | **Cool Slate** | Reduces eye strain compared to pure white; gives a "FinTech" feel. |
| **Surface** | `#FFFFFF` | **Pure White** | Card interiors and content areas for crisp data separation. |
| **Deep Text** | `#0F172A` | **Midnight Navy** | Headers and primary body text; provides superior contrast. |
| **Muted Text** | `#64748B` | **Slate Grey** | Metadata and secondary info to reduce visual noise. |

---

## 🧠 Why This Palette Was Chosen

### 1. The Psychology of Trust
Blue is the industry standard for auditing, finance, and enterprise software. It represents stability, intelligence, and professional reliability—qualities central to a valuation system.

### 2. Premium Tech Aesthetic
By using a **Cool Slate** background (`#F1F5F9`) instead of standard grey, the UI feels more modern and expensive. This "off-white" approach creates a soft, premium backdrop that makes data-heavy tables feel less cluttered and more scannable.

### 3. Visual Hierarchy & Accessibility
The stark difference between the **Midnight Navy** text and the **Cool Slate** background ensures that even small text remains highly readable, ensuring compliance with high-contrast accessibility standards while maintaining a professional look.

## ✨ How It Manages Color Harmony

### Monochromatic Base
80% of the UI exists on a monochromatic scale of blues and slates. This ensures that the core interface always feels unified and never clashes with itself, regardless of how many tabs or windows are open.

### Split-Complementary Accents
While the UI is "cool," we use high-saturation status colors for critical feedback. These colors are specifically tuned to stand out against the blue base without breaking harmony:
- **Success (`#16A34A`)**: Saturated Leaf Green.
- **Error (`#DC2626`)**: Vibrant Ruby Red.
- **Warning (`#D97706`)**: Amber Orange.

### Saturation Synchronization
Even though these status colors are Green, Red, and Orange, they all share a similar **vibrancy and brightness level** with the Primary Blue. This prevents "color vibration" (where one color feels significantly louder than another), maintaining a balanced visual weight across the dashboard.

### Adaptive Harmony
The project includes a parallel **Dark Mode** that maintains this harmony by shifting the base from Slate to **Deep Space Navy** (`#0F172A`), ensuring the visual identity remains consistent and eye-friendly in low-light environments.
